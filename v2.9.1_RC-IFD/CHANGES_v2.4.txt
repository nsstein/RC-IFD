RC-IFD v2.4 — navigation-display traffic overlay fixed  (web/index.html only; backend unchanged)

Problem in v2.3 (reproduced): only amber/red targets were pinned. Every other aircraft in the traffic list that fell
outside the map area was silently dropped — with the map at 1 km, 4 of 7 listed aircraft at 3–9.5 km were invisible.
The few pins that were drawn landed on the top edge under the heading box and could stack on top of each other.

v2.4:
  * EVERY aircraft in the traffic list (inside Display range and the ± height band — the same limits that drive the
    TRAFFIC list and the alerts) appears on the navigation display.
  * Inside the compass section (ring, outside the ring, corners): drawn at its true scaled position.
  * Beyond the section at the current map range: pinned to the section edge on its true bearing, with an arrow
    pointing to the real position and a label — distance and relative height (± m, ↑/↓), plus callsign for
    cyan / amber / red. White (non-alerting) pins are slightly smaller and dimmer.
  * Pins are laid out around the whole perimeter: red/amber are placed first and keep their exact bearing; others take
    the nearest free slot (wrapping round corners if needed). The heading box is kept clear. Labels are only drawn
    where they do not overlap — red gets up to four positions to find room. If more pins than fit, the count shows
    "N OFF-SCALE (+k in TRAFFIC list)" in amber.
  * Traffic is now drawn last, on top of the map, compass and data blocks.
  * Rings: amber = horizontal alert radius (TA), red = threat radius (half), dotted grey = Display range limit.
  * Auto-range (unchanged behaviour): zooms out until amber/red traffic is at its true position, label "TFC x km".
Tested in the simulator with injected traffic: heading-up and north-up, auto-range on/off, 19-target stress test
(min 28 px between symbols), desktop / tablet / phone layouts — every listed target drawn, no JS errors.
